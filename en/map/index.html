Looty Bay map: Fences, Smuggler's Vans, bank and police

Looty Bay has 4 Fences ($ on the map) and 2 Smuggler's Vans (the green box). You sell items to a Fence and cargo at a Smuggler's Van. Looty Bay Bank is in the north of Downtown, and the Police Station is in Palmside.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in Early Access and changes every week.

What's where

Tap a place in the list or an icon on the map: every place of that kind gets a red circle.

Where to sell items: the Fence

  • There are 4 Fences on the map (the $ icon): on the east shore of Loraine Hills, on the south pier of Sunny Beach and two in Downtown.
  • The Fence pays cash. How much depends on the value of the loot, your Notoriety and your item sale bonus.
  • Cash on you can be taken, but money in the bank can't be stolen: how to open an account and launder money.
  • Don't sell uncommon decoders to the Fence: you need them for the barter (decoder calculator).

Where to sell cargo: the Smuggler's Van

  • There are 2 vans on the map (the green box): in Sunny Beach and in the east of Downtown.
  • The payout depends on how much cargo you bring, how valuable it is and your cargo sale bonus. Delivering in bulk pays more, but it's risky.
  • You can also sell cargo at the Sales Center of your district if you rent a plot there. More: what to do with cargo.

Bank and Police Station

  • Looty Bay Bank is in the north of Downtown. That's where you open an account for $5,000: how to open an account.
  • The Police Station is in Palmside. That's where you go on duty: how to become a cop.

Grand Hotel and Art Gallery

  • The Grand Hotel is in Sunny Beach. A Keycard opens its secret warehouse.
  • The Art Gallery is in the south of Palmside. It has a vault, and exhibits can be stolen off their pedestals.

Districts of Looty Bay

  • Loraine Hills: the north of the west island.
  • Sunny Beach: the south of the west island.
  • Palmside: the middle island.
  • Downtown: the east island.
