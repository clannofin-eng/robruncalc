Bank account and money laundering in ROB RUN

Go to Looty Bay Bank as a Civilian (no mask) with $5,000 in cash and choose Open Account at the bank employee. Then put dirty cash on the Laundering Desk at your home: it launders on its own, and Transfer To My Account moves the money to the bank, where nobody can steal it. The desk is the only way to top up the account.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in Early Access and changes every week.

How many years will my money give?

Open an account

  1. Get $5,000 in cash.
  2. Enter Looty Bay without a mask, as a Civilian.
  3. Go to Looty Bay Bank in the north of Downtown (see the map below).
  4. Talk to the bank employee and choose Open Account for $5,000.

The account unlocks:

  • cash laundering;
  • secure account storage and cash withdrawals;
  • currency exchange for B-Coins and cash;
  • clan creation and real estate investments.

Launder and top up

  1. You need a home: rent a plot with a Land Plot Title (prices).
  2. Put dirty cash on the Laundering Desk and choose Start Laundering. It keeps going while you're offline.
  3. Press Transfer To My Account: the clean credits go to the bank.

The game says it plainly: you can only top up your account using the laundering desk at your home.

Launder faster

  • The rate depends on your settlement's level, the desk's modifications and the businesses your clan controls.
  • Modifications such as the Banknote Counter ($30,000, $65,000 or $150,000) and the Shell Company speed it up, and bigger amounts get a volume bonus.
  • The desk has a laundering limit: when it's full, transfer to the account first.
  • You can also launder at Lenny's Motel, but Lenny takes a cut. At your own base there's no fee.
  • A settlement's owner collects a tax when residents launder cash there.

Withdrawals, bribes and B-Coin

  • Withdraw at any ATM or from a bank employee. The money becomes dirty cash again, and anyone can take it off you.
  • Credits in the account can't be stolen. Clan operations are paid in credits.
  • An arrested player pays the Police a bribe straight from the account.
  • B-Coin is bought with credits in the currency exchange. The bank employee says even the feds can't touch it during a seizure.

What the federal raid takes

At the wipe the feds confiscate the bank account too: it's the “Bank Account” line of the court ruling and counts in full, like dirty cash on you, in boxes or on the desk. See how many years your money gives: jail time calculator.

FAQ

How much does a bank account cost?

$5,000 in cash, paid at Looty Bay Bank.

Can I open an account as a Criminal?

No. Come in as a Civilian, without a mask.

Can someone steal money from my account?

No. Only cash can be stolen.

Why can't I deposit money?

You can only top up the account through the Laundering Desk at your home, and you need an open account to launder.
