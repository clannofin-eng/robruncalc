How much sentence it adds in a raid

Unlocked shipments count toward your sentence at 100% of their research cost. Every full $500,000 is 1 year, up to 40 years per state.

This shipment costs $320,000: that’s 64% of one year of sentence. The whole path to it costs $495,000: less than one year of sentence before your other assets.

The sentence counts everything you own at once: money, cars, property and shipments add up. The calculator gives the exact number.
