How to unlock Electronic Fuse (protection 5)

Together with this shipment that’s 3 shipments for $660,000.

How much sentence it adds in a raid

Unlocked shipments count toward your sentence at 100% of their research cost. Every full $500,000 is 1 year, up to 40 years per state.

This shipment costs $500,000: that’s 1 year of sentence. The whole path to it costs $660,000: that’s 1 year of sentence before your other assets.

The item itself counts separately once you buy it: 40% of its price, that is $100,000.

The sentence counts everything you own at once: money, cars, property and shipments add up. The calculator gives the exact number.
