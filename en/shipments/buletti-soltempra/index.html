How to unlock Buletti Soltempra

  1. Fukurou Tensa$50,000

  3. Longhorn Wild$500,000

Together with this shipment that’s 4 shipments for $2,750,000.

How much sentence it adds in a raid

Unlocked shipments count toward your sentence at 100% of their research cost. Every full $500,000 is 1 year, up to 40 years per state.

This shipment costs $2,000,000: that’s 4 years of sentence. The whole path to it costs $2,750,000: that’s 5 years of sentence before your other assets.

The item itself counts separately once you buy it: 40% of its price, that is $4,000,000.

The sentence counts everything you own at once: money, cars, property and shipments add up. The calculator gives the exact number.
