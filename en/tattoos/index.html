ROB RUN tattoos: how much jail time each one needs

The Tattoo Artist in prison inks a tattoo once your total jail time reaches its mark. There are 23 of them, the Trust No One set on the Authority screen. The first, Chance, comes at 1 year, so everyone gets it at the start of the game. The last, Revolvers, unlocks at 800 years together with the Silence mask. Jail time and tattoos survive the wipe. Below you can see each tattoo on the character from the game.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in Early Access and changes every week.

Tattoos on the character

Tap a tattoo in the list and it appears on the character. Turn him with the buttons or a swipe.

Your total is shown at the top right of the Authority screen at the Tattoo Artist.

All 23 tattoos in order

The order and names are the ones on the Authority screen in the game. The two Eagles are different tattoos: one at 60 years, one at 120.

How to get a tattoo

  1. Go to prison: you end up there after the federal raid or when you surrender to the feds. The sentence from each state adds to your total: how jail time is counted.
  2. Talk to the Tattoo Artist in prison: he opens the Authority screen with every tattoo.
  3. Pick a tattoo and press “Get Inked”. If your jail time is short, the button shows the years needed in red.

How many states to 800 years

One state gives at most 40 years, and since 17 November 2026 no more than 40 years a day. Starting from 1 year, reaching 800 takes 20 states at 40 years each. Paying Lenny's debt adds +10 years above the cap: details.

Tattoos and the wipe

The federal raid takes everything you own in the state, but not your jail time: it adds to your total, and your tattoos stay with you. When your state wipes.

FAQ

How many tattoos are there in ROB RUN?

Twenty-three, all from the Trust No One set. The last one, Revolvers, unlocks at 800 years of jail time, and the Silence mask comes with it.

Where do I get a tattoo?

From the Tattoo Artist in prison, on the Authority screen. Once your total jail time reaches a tattoo's mark, press “Get Inked”.

Where is each tattoo on the body?

All over: Chance on the right upper arm, the Crown and both Eagles on the chest, Defiance and Revolvers on the back, Power on the back of the head, O.G. on the left cheek. Tap a tattoo in the list above and the character turns to show it.

Does a tattoo cost jail time?

No. A tattoo unlocks when your total reaches its mark, and the total doesn't go down: with 83 years all six tattoos up to 80 years are unlocked.

How much jail time does the Silence mask need?

800 years in total, the same as the last tattoo, Revolvers.

Do I lose my tattoos in the wipe?

No. Your total jail time and tattoos carry over; only what you owned in the state is gone.
