How to change server and play with friends in ROB RUN

Servers in ROB RUN are called states, and you can only party up inside one state. To move, surrender to the feds (Settings → Other → Surrender to Feds), then in prison go to an Exit point and pick your friend's state in State Selection. Surrendering confiscates everything you own in the current state and gives you a sentence.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in Early Access and changes every week.

How many years will surrendering give me?

Change state step by step

  1. Press Esc and open Settings → Other. Choose Surrender to Feds and confirm.
  2. Everything you own in the current state is confiscated and you go to prison with a sentence.
  3. In prison, walk to an Exit point and press Escape.
  4. In the Prison Break window, escape to the nearest state or choose one yourself.
  5. In State Selection, find the state by its ID or in the list. Population shows how full it is (Low, Medium, Large, Full), Lifetime shows how old it is, and [New] marks fresh ones. Show only servers with friends keeps the states where your Steam friends play.

Before you switch

  • Everything you own in the state is confiscated: house, land, cars, money, cargo. See how many years that gives: jail time calculator.
  • Your progress in the current state is lost, and you leave your clan.
  • Unclaimed rewards in your mail are lost, so collect them first.
  • In an old state other players can be far ahead of you. A fresh one ([New]) is easier to start in.
  • Check how long the new state has left until the federal raid: wipe timer.

How to play with friends

  • You have to be in the same region and the same state. The region is changed in Settings → Other, and only while you're in prison.
  • A party holds up to 3 players. Invite in Party Management (Esc → Party) or from the Steam Friends tab.
  • To enter a location together, every party member must be on the global map.
  • In Looty Bay the whole party plays one role: all Criminals, all Police or all Civilians.
  • For a bigger crew, make a clan: it holds up to 100 players in one state. Creating a clan needs a bank account: how to open one.
  • Playing alone or as a pair has a bonus: Solo Mode (Hard) starts you at Notoriety level 4 with better chances of extra rare loot, and Duo Mode at level 2 with more profit from selling cargo and items.

FAQ

Why can't I invite my friend?

Your friend is in another state or region. One of you has to surrender to the feds and escape to the other's state.

How many players can be in a party?

Three, you included. Bigger groups use a clan.

Will I lose my jail time if I change server?

No. Jail time only grows: surrendering adds a new sentence to your total.

Can I take my base to another state?

No. Everything in the old state is confiscated when you surrender.
