How to become a cop in ROB RUN

Enter Looty Bay as a Civilian, go to the Police Station and choose Start Duty at the officer's desk. You get free gear and a service car, but you can't use your personal items on shift. If all police slots are taken, wait or accept a Police Backup invite: it comes with free equipment and a salary bonus.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in Early Access and changes every week.

How to start duty

  1. Enter Looty Bay without a mask, as a Civilian.
  2. Go to the Police Station in Palmside (see the map below).
  3. Talk to the officer at the desk and choose Start Duty. The police have a limited number of slots.
  4. Pick your equipment: the Patrol Officer set is free, and a badge gives a rank's equipment once.
  5. Take a service car: Longhorn Guard, Longhorn Outrider (off-road SUV) or Longhorn Wild Guard (muscle car).
  6. To finish, choose End Duty. The equipment you have on is lost.

Pay

  • Lawful actions put money into your account. Each shift has an earnings limit.
  • At the end of each shift you get Confiscated Goods worth what you earned.
  • An arrest pays much more than a kill. Ordering a Criminal to surrender gives a temporary bonus to the arrest reward, and so does an active siren on your service car.
  • A “Police department expansion! Salary bonus” invite gives free equipment and a higher salary for a while.

Badges and equipment

  • Without a badge you work as a Patrol Officer. That free equipment gets better as the state gets older.
  • The Sergeant's Badge, Lieutenant's Badge and Captain's Badge each let you take that rank's equipment once while on duty. The ranks go Sergeant I and II, Lieutenant I and II, Captain.
  • Badges are found around the city.
  • If you die on duty you get medical help, but you need a new badge to take badge equipment again. You don't lose your inventory.
  • Leave Looty Bay before the feds arrive (the “Federal raid in” timer) and the badge you used is returned.

Rules on duty

  • The Police can only kill Criminals; Criminals can also kill each other.
  • You can arrest Criminals who surrender or are knocked out. The arrested Criminal loses the bag but keeps the equipment.
  • The Police can confiscate Criminals' bags and cargo.
  • An arrested player can offer you a bribe straight from their bank account.
  • The Police can close the way out of Looty Bay by blocking the bridges, and can ram a Criminal's car.
  • Traffic violations (running people over, vandalism) send Police and Civilians to the city detention center for a while.

FAQ

Why can't I become a cop?

All police slots are taken. Wait for one to free up, or accept a Police Backup invite when the department needs officers.

Do cops lose their items when they die?

No, the Police don't lose their inventory on death. But badge equipment needs a new badge after you die.

How do cops get paid?

During the shift, money for lawful actions goes to your account; at the end of the shift you get Confiscated Goods worth the amount earned.

Can a cop use their own weapons?

No. On duty you use free state equipment; personal items are off limits.
