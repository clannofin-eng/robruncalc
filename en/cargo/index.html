What to do with cargo in ROB RUN

Sell cargo for cash at the Smuggler's Van in Looty Bay or at the Sales Center of the district where you rent a plot, or take it home and open it at a Cargo Workbench to get what's inside. Contraband bought from Lenny or a Sales Center is worth much more after you repack it at a Contraband Workbench. A higher Notoriety level raises the price.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in Early Access and changes every week.

Cargo prices

Where to sell it and what else to do

  • Smuggler's Van in Looty Bay: there are two, in Sunny Beach and in Downtown (map below). On the global map it's “Cargo Sale”. The payout depends on how much cargo you bring and how valuable it is. Delivering in bulk pays much more, but it's risky.
  • Sales Center in your district: only players who rent a plot in that district can sell there, and the district's level raises the price.
  • Open it at home on a Cargo Workbench ($15,000). Locksmith Tools, the Cargo Saw and the Bench Vise speed it up and add slots.
  • Repack contraband: Contraband Cargo bought from Lenny or at a Sales Center is worth significantly more after the Contraband Workbench ($20,000).
  • Collections: the Collection Table ($15,000) packs stolen items into a Collection Crate that the Smuggler buys.
  • Every delivery to the Smuggler also fills the Cargo Sheet, which sells for a good price once full.

Carrying cargo

  • If you draw a weapon while carrying cargo, you drop it.
  • The car trunk keeps cargo apart from your other belongings; at home, store it on racks.
  • Cargo dropped on the ground disappears after a while.
  • The Police can confiscate a Criminal's cargo, and a Criminal who dies loses the whole inventory.

Cargo and the federal raid

At the wipe the feds take your cargo too, and it counts in full at its value. See how many years your cargo adds: jail time calculator.

FAQ

Where do I sell cargo in ROB RUN?

At a Smuggler's Van in Looty Bay (there are two: in Sunny Beach and in Downtown), or at the Sales Center of your district if you rent a plot there.

What is the Cargo Workbench for?

It opens stolen cargo at your base so you get the items inside. Its upgrades open cargo faster and hold more of it.

Does Notoriety change cargo prices?

Yes. A high Notoriety level significantly raises the price of cargo sold to the Smuggler, and Duo Mode starts you with more profit from cargo.
