How jail time is calculated in ROB RUN

In short: in a raid the feds add up the value of everything you own in the state, and every full $500,000 becomes 1 year of jail time, up to 40 years per state. Property you bought (house, buildings, land, vehicles, upgrades and fuses) counts at 40% of its purchase price. Money, shipment research, bushes and cargo count in full. Paying off Lenny’s debt adds another 10 years above the cap.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in early access and the rules can change.

Calculate your jail time

When you get jail time

Every 7 days the feds raid: everything you own in the state is seized and turned into years. The same happens if you surrender to the feds earlier: all property in your current state is confiscated.

The formula

  1. Add up everything that gets seized, by its share: bought property at 40% of its price, everything else in full.
  2. Divide the total by $500,000 and drop the fraction: anything under $500,000 left over doesn’t count.
  3. You can’t get more than 40 years for one state, which is $20,000,000 counted.
  4. If Lenny’s debt is paid, add 10 years on top of those 40.

The 40% for real estate, land and vehicles is checked against a real in-game court ruling (below), and the game itself values a bought car at $80,000 when it cost $200,000. The other shares are a player model; the game doesn’t publish them. Dirty cash counts too, wherever it is: on you, in boxes or on the laundering table.

Limits

  • No more than 40 years for one state.
  • Since 17 November 2026, no more than 40 years per 24 hours (bonus years don’t count toward this limit).
  • No more than 1,200 years on one character, bonus years included.

Lenny’s debt: +10 years

Paying Lenny $1,000,000 in clean money at the motel gives a one-time +10 years above the 40-year cap. The same $1,000,000 seized in a raid would give only 2 years.

Frequently asked questions

How many years do I get for $1,000,000?

If it’s money, shipment research or cargo, 2 years. If it’s the price of property you bought (a house, a car, land), $400,000 counts, which is less than a year.

How much do I need for 40 years?

$20,000,000 counted. That’s $20,000,000 in money or $50,000,000 in the price of bought property, and usually a mix of both.

Does my car count toward the sentence?

Yes, at 40% of its purchase price, on the “Vehicles” line. A Fukurou Tensa bought for $200,000 shows up in the ruling as $80,000.

Does anything under $500,000 count?

No. $999,999 counted gives 1 year; $1,000,000 already gives 2.

What does Lenny’s debt give?

Paying the $1,000,000 debt in clean money gives a one-time +10 years above the 40-year cap.

What is the longest sentence?

40 years per state plus 10 for Lenny’s debt. On top of that, no more than 40 years per 24 hours and no more than 1,200 years per character.

How do I calculate my sentence quickly?

Use the calculator at robruncalc.com: mark the shipments you’ve unlocked, choose your house, car and the rest in “Your base”, and enter your money. It counts by the same rules as the game.
