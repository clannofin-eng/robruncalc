When is the wipe in ROB RUN

There's no single wipe day. Every state (server) lives 7 days, then the federal raid confiscates everything in it and turns it into jail time. Your jail time, cosmetics and name carry over. Enter the “Server lifetime” from the main menu and the timer shows the exact date and time of the raid in your time zone.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in Early Access and changes every week.

Raid timer

In the main menu the time goes up: “Server lifetime: 3h 1m”, a minute later 3h 2m, a day later 1d 3h 1m. Enter it as it is. “Time left” is for the State Selection tooltip “Less than … remaining until the federal raid”.

How the wipe works

  • Each state is raided 7 days after it opens. Kefir says the cycle length may change.
  • New states open every day: on their own when the old ones fill up, and on a schedule shown on the State Selection screen.
  • In the raid the feds take everything you own in the state: house, land, cars, cash and the bank account, cargo. Its value becomes years of jail time: how jail time is counted.
  • Before the raid the game warns that the “federal confiscation procedure has been initiated!” and shows how long is left until confiscation.

What you keep after the wipe

  • Your total jail time and the Tattoo Artist's tattoos for it.
  • Cosmetics, your name, tutorial progress and settings.
  • B-Coin: the bank employee in the game says even the feds can't touch it during a seizure.
  • Everything else in the state is gone; you get jail time instead.

Don't confuse it with the Looty Bay timer

Inside the city the game counts down “Federal raid in …”. That's the city closing, not the state wipe: leave before the feds arrive. A Criminal who stays loses all gear and items; a Police Officer gets the used badge back.

How to get ready

  • Count your sentence ahead: you can't get more than 40 years per state, which is $20,000,000 counted. Anything above that adds no years, so spend it: jail time calculator.
  • Paying Lenny's debt adds +10 years above the cap: details.
  • Want a new state sooner? Surrender to the feds and pick a fresh one: how to change server.

FAQ

Does everyone wipe at the same time?

No. Every state has its own 7-day timer from the moment it opened. States open every day, so some state wipes every day.

Do I lose my jail time in the wipe?

No. The sentence from each raid adds to your total jail time, which unlocks the Tattoo Artist's rewards, new shipments and parts of the prison.

Where does the game show when the raid is?

In the main menu: “Server lifetime” shows how long the state has existed, for example 3h 1m or 1d 3h 1m. The raid comes when it reaches 7 days, so 7 days minus that time from now. In the State Selection list, older states have a tooltip “Less than … remaining until the federal raid”.
