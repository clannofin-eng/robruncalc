ROB RUN roulette calculator

The roulette at Lenny's Motel is a European wheel with one zero: 37 numbers from 0 to 36. A number pays 35 to 1, a split 17 to 1, a street 11 to 1, a corner 8 to 1, a six line 5 to 1, a dozen or a column 2 to 1, and red or black, odd or even, 1–18 or 19–36 pay 1 to 1. Zero beats every outside bet, so on average the table keeps 2.7% of every bet. Put chips on the table below to see what each number would pay you.

How to play roulette in ROB RUN

  1. The roulette table is in the bar at Lenny's Motel.
  2. Press Tab (Exchange) and buy chips for cash from the dealer: 1K, 5K, 25K, 50K and 100K.
  3. While the timer runs, the left mouse button places a chip (Place Bet), the right one takes it back (Cancel Bet), and R clears all your bets. Hover over a spot and the game shows what it pays.
  4. When the timer ends, the game says “All bets set” and spins the wheel. Several players bet on the same spin.
  5. If you win, collect your winnings from the dealer and exchange the chips back for cash.

The limit is 200K per bet: the game won't take more and says “Bet limit reached”.

Can you predict the roulette?

  • No. Every spin is a new draw of 37 numbers: past results don't change the next one, and no colour is “due”.
  • The game's tip “the more accurate your prediction, the higher your winnings” means that narrow bets pay more, not that you can guess the result.
  • No betting system beats the 2.7% edge: it only changes how often you win and how much you risk.
  • Martingale (doubling your red or black bet after every loss) wins small amounts often and loses a lot rarely. The 200K bet limit also cuts the doubling short: starting from 1K, 8 losses in a row cost you 255K, and a 256K bet isn't allowed. Check it with your own numbers:

FAQ

What does each roulette bet pay in ROB RUN?

A number pays 35 to 1, a split 17 to 1, a street or a trio with zero 11 to 1, a corner or the first four (0, 1, 2, 3) 8 to 1, a six line 5 to 1, a dozen or a column 2 to 1, and red/black, odd/even and 1–18/19–36 pay 1 to 1.

What is the bet limit in ROB RUN roulette?

200K per bet: the game won't take more and says “Bet limit reached”. Because of the limit, a Martingale that starts at 1K ends after 8 losses in a row.

Is it European or American roulette?

European: one zero and no double zero, so the house edge is 2.7%, not 5.26%.

What is the best bet?

In the long run every bet loses the same 2.7%. Red or black wins most often (18 of 37 numbers) but pays least; a single number pays 35 to 1 but comes up once in 37 spins on average.

Can I predict the next number?

No. Spins don't depend on each other, so past numbers tell you nothing about the next one.
