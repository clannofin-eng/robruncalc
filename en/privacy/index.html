Privacy policy

robruncalc.com is a free fan site for ROB RUN, run by one independent author. It is not connected with KEFIR. This page explains what data the site uses and why. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

What the site does not collect

  • There are no accounts or sign-ups, and the site never asks for your name or game login.
  • Your calculator data (the tree, your base, the amounts) stays in your browser: in the page address and in the browser’s local storage. It is not sent to us.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

We count visits with Google Analytics 4 to see how many players come, from which countries and which pages they use. Google receives your IP address, your browser and device type and the pages you open, and sets _ga cookies. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland these cookies are only set after you press “Allow”; you can change your choice with the “Cookies” button at the bottom of the page.

How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • The site shows ads from Google AdSense to pay for itself.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of other vendors’ cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • For visitors from the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, personalized ads are shown only with their consent.

Support form

If you write to us through the support form, your message, the contact you leave (optional), the screenshots you attach, the page address, your calculation link (if you tick it) and your browser version are sent to the author by e-mail through Google Apps Script. We use them only to answer you and fix the site, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your rights and contact

You can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time; the calculator works without them. To ask about your data or have a support message deleted, write through the Support button on the calculator page.

The site is not directed at children under 13.
