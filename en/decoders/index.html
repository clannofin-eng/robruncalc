How many decoders you need in ROB RUN

Every building on a base has its own Security System. Its defense is the building's base defense plus the fuses in its slots (+1, +3 or +5 each). A Decoder lowers the defense by its breaching: uncommon 1, epic 3, legendary 5. Divide the defense by the breaching and round up: a small house without fuses takes 15 uncommon, 5 epic or 3 legendary decoders.

Updated 24 November 2026. The game is in Early Access and changes every week.

How breaching works

  • Each building has its own Security System: the house, a garage, a warehouse and a fence are breached separately.
  • Defense = the building's base defense + fuses. An Electronic Fuse adds +1 (uncommon), +3 (epic) or +5 (legendary). In the game these are the “Basic” and “Additional” lines of the defense level tooltip.
  • The Car Garage and the Warehouse also have 3 “Special Modules” slots besides the fuse slots.
  • A decoder is used up, and placing one takes about 12 seconds. You can mix rarities.
  • Once the defense reaches zero, all doors, gates and windows of that building open for a while.
  • A breach starts a raid block: some actions inside the base are locked for a while, for example you can't rebuild or move it.
  • The farm fence has no Security System.

How to get decoders

  • Find them in Looty Bay: in cabinets, safes and containers. Legendary ones are rare.
  • Barter at the Suspicious Door in Lenny's Motel: 3 uncommon decoders plus $10,000 for 1 epic decoder.
  • A filled Action Camera can also be bartered for an epic decoder. The camera fills as you commit crimes at the Notoriety level it asks for.
  • Decoders aren't sold on the Black Market.
  • Don't sell uncommon decoders to the Fence: you need them for the barter.

How to protect your base

  • Put Electronic Fuses into the Security System slots of each building. On the Black Market they cost $20,000, $100,000 and $250,000 once you research the shipments: protection 1, protection 3, protection 5.
  • Give the best fuses to the buildings that hold your loot and cash.
  • Fuses aren't destroyed in a breach: repair the building and the defense comes back.
  • In the federal raid fuses count at 40% of their price, like everything else you bought: count your sentence.

FAQ

How many decoders for a small house?

Without fuses: 15 uncommon, 5 epic or 3 legendary. With legendary fuses in all 27 slots its defense is 150: 50 epic or 30 legendary decoders.

Can I mix decoders?

Yes. Each one lowers the defense by its own breaching. Enter how many of each you have and the calculator picks the shortest set.

Where can I buy a decoder?

Nowhere on the Black Market. You find decoders in Looty Bay or barter for them at the Suspicious Door in Lenny's Motel.

Do fuses break when someone raids me?

No. After a breach you can repair the building and its defense comes back.
